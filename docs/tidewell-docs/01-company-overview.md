# Tidewell Logistics: Company Overview

**Last updated:** 12 January 2026 | **Owner:** People & Operations

## About us
Tidewell Logistics B.V. is a freight-forwarding and last-mile delivery company founded in 2016 in Rotterdam. Customers book shipments and follow parcels in real time through our platform, Tidewell Track.

## Locations
- **Rotterdam HQ (Netherlands):** about 140 employees.
- **Lisbon Hub (Portugal):** opened in 2021, about 72 employees across operations, support and engineering.

## Teams
Operations, Engineering, Customer Success, and Finance & People.

## Working hours and core hours
Company-wide core hours are **10:00 to 15:00 Rotterdam time (CET/CEST)**. Outside core hours, people choose their own schedule in agreement with their manager. Lisbon is on Western European Time, which is one hour behind Rotterdam. Meetings that include both offices must be scheduled inside core hours.

## Communication
- Slack is for anything internal. Email is for anything external.
- During core hours, Slack messages addressed to you should be answered within two working hours.
- The monthly all-hands takes place on the first Thursday of each month at 16:00 Rotterdam time.
