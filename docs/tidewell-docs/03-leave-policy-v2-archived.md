# Leave Policy (Version 2): ARCHIVED

> **SUPERSEDED on 31 December 2025 by Leave Policy v3.** Kept for audit purposes only. Do not use for current entitlements.

**Effective:** 1 January 2024 to 31 December 2025 | **Owner:** People team

## 1. Annual leave (v2)
- Rotterdam employees: 23 days per calendar year.
- Lisbon employees: 20 days per calendar year, plus Portuguese public holidays.

## 2. Carry-over (v2)
- Up to 10 unused days could be carried over and had to be used by 30 April.

## 3. Sick leave (v2)
- Notify your manager before 09:00 on the first day of absence.
- A doctor's note was required after 2 consecutive days of sickness.
- Sick pay was 100% for the first 6 weeks.

## 4. Carer's leave (v2)
- 3 paid days per calendar year.

## 5. Parental leave (v2)
- Primary caregiver: 12 weeks at full pay.
- Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks at full pay.

## 6. Unpaid leave (v2)
- Up to 2 weeks of unpaid leave with Head of Department approval.
