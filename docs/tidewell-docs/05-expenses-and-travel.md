# Expenses and Business Travel Policy

**Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Owner:** Finance

## 1. Daily allowances and limits

| Item | Limit |
|---|---|
| Meal allowance, domestic travel with overnight stay | EUR 35 per day |
| Meal allowance, international travel with overnight stay | EUR 50 per day |
| Hotel, standard nightly cap | EUR 140 |
| Hotel, Zurich, London, New York, Singapore | EUR 200 |
| Mileage, personal car | EUR 0.23 per km |
| Client entertainment | EUR 60 per person, needs prior approval from Head of Department |

Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## 2. Flights
- Economy class for flights shorter than 6 hours.
- Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer.
- Business class is never allowed without written CFO approval.

## 3. Receipts and deadlines
- A receipt is required for **every expense over EUR 15**.
- Submit claims within **30 days** of the expense.
- Claims 31 to 60 days old need a written note from your manager.
- Claims older than 60 days are rejected.

## 4. Approval thresholds
| Claim amount | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to EUR 250 | Line manager |
| EUR 250.01 to EUR 2,000 | Head of Department |
| Above EUR 2,000 | CFO (Pieter de Vries) |

## 5. Related policies
- Home office equipment and allowances: see the Hybrid and Remote Work Policy.
- Conference tickets and courses are charged to the employee's learning budget (see Benefits and Compensation). Travel to the event is still claimed under this policy.

## 6. Exceptions
- **Lisbon Freight Summit:** during the first full week of October, the hotel cap for Lisbon is raised to **EUR 190** per night because of local price surges.
