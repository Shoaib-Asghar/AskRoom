# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

**Last updated:** 20 January 2026 | **Owner:** People team

## Before day one
- Your laptop is shipped so that it arrives at least 3 working days before your start date.
- You will be assigned an onboarding buddy who is not your manager.

## Day one
- 09:30: welcome session with the People team.
- Laptop handover and account setup with IT. You will set up the password manager and multi-factor authentication.
- Lunch with your team and buddy.

## Week one
- Meet your manager to agree your 30/60/90-day goals.
- Complete these mandatory trainings within your first **2 weeks**:
  - Security awareness (1 hour)
  - Data protection / GDPR (90 minutes)
  - Health and safety for warehouse visits (30 minutes)

## Week two
- Shadow colleagues in your team and in one other team.
- Claim your home office equipment allowance if applicable (deadline: 3 months from your start date).

## Goal reviews
Your manager reviews progress with you at day 30, day 60 and day 90. A formal probation review takes place in month 5.

## Probation and notice
- Probation lasts **6 months**.
- During probation, the notice period is **1 month** for both sides.
- After probation, the notice period is **3 months** for both sides.

## Questions
Email people@tidewell.example or ask your buddy.
