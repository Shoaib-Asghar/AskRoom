# Leave Policy (Version 3)

**Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Supersedes:** Leave Policy v2 (2024) | **Owner:** People team

## 1. Annual leave
- **Rotterdam employees:** 25 days per calendar year (full-time).
- **Lisbon employees:** 22 days per calendar year (full-time), plus Portuguese public holidays.
- Part-time employees receive a pro-rated allowance.
- Public holidays in the employee's country of employment are not deducted from the allowance.
- In the first year of employment, leave is pro-rated by the number of full months worked.

## 2. Requesting and carrying over leave
- Absences of up to 5 consecutive days need at least 1 week of notice.
- Absences of more than 5 consecutive days need at least 4 weeks of notice.
- Up to **5 unused days** may be carried into the next year and must be used by **31 March**. Anything above 5 days, or carried-over days not used by 31 March, is forfeited.
- Unused leave is only paid out when employment ends.

## 3. Sick leave
- Notify your manager **before 09:30** on the first day of absence, by phone or Slack DM.
- A medical certificate is required **from the 4th consecutive day** of sickness.
- Sick pay is 100% for the first 8 weeks in any rolling 12-month period and 70% thereafter, up to 52 weeks.

## 4. Carer's leave
- **5 paid days per calendar year** to care for a dependent or close relative who is ill or needs urgent care.
- Carer's leave is separate from annual leave and from sick leave.
- It can be taken in half days. Tell your manager as soon as possible.

## 5. Parental leave
- **Primary caregiver:** 16 weeks at full pay.
- **Secondary caregiver:** 6 weeks at full pay, to be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
- Please notify the People team at least 12 weeks before the expected date where possible.

## 6. Unpaid leave and sabbaticals
- Up to 4 weeks of unpaid leave can be approved by your Head of Department. Longer periods need approval from the Head of People.
- Sabbatical leave is covered in the Benefits and Compensation guide.
