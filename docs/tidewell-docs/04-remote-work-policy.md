# Hybrid and Remote Work Policy

**Effective:** 1 March 2025 | **Owner:** People team

## 1. Office attendance
Tidewell works on a hybrid model.
- **Rotterdam:** at least **2 office days per week**. Tuesday and Thursday are anchor days.
- **Lisbon:** at least **3 office days per week**, because the hub shares its building with warehouse operations. Tuesday and Thursday are also anchor days.
- Roles hired as fully remote are agreed in the employment contract and are not covered by these minimums.

## 2. Availability
Everyone working remotely must be reachable during company core hours (see the Company Overview).

## 3. Working from abroad
Employees may work from another country for a limited time under these conditions:
- Allowed countries: EU/EEA member states, Switzerland and the United Kingdom only.
- Maximum of **20 working days per calendar year** in total.
- Maximum of **10 consecutive working days** per trip.
- Request at least **14 days in advance**. Both your manager and the People team must approve.
- Working from any other country is **not permitted**, for tax and insurance reasons.

## 4. Home office setup
- **One-off equipment allowance of EUR 600** (desk, chair, monitor or similar). It must be claimed within 3 months of the start date.
- **EUR 40 per month** internet and phone allowance, paid with salary.
- Coworking space costs are not reimbursed unless approved in advance by your Head of Department.

## 5. Equipment
A company laptop is provided separately and does not count towards the equipment allowance. See the IT and Security guide.
