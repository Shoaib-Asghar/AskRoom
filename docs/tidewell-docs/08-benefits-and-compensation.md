# Benefits and Compensation Guide

**Effective:** 1 January 2026 | **Owner:** People team

## Pension
Tidewell matches employee pension contributions up to **6% of gross salary**. Enrolment starts on the first day of the month after joining.

## Learning budget
- **EUR 1,200 per calendar year** for courses, books and conference tickets. Manager approval is required.
- Pro-rated by month for people who join during the year.
- Unused budget does **not** roll over into the next year.
- Travel and accommodation for a conference are not part of the learning budget. Claim them under the Expenses and Business Travel Policy.

## Wellbeing
- EUR 25 per month towards gym or fitness memberships.
- Bike lease scheme (Rotterdam only).

## Performance bonus
- Target bonus is **8% of annual base salary**, paid in **March** for the previous calendar year. Actual payout ranges from 0% to 150% of target.
- To be eligible for a given performance year, an employee must have **started before 1 October of that year**.
- Contractors and interns are not eligible.

## Salary review and promotions
- Salary reviews happen once a year in **April**, effective 1 May.
- Promotions are considered in the **October** cycle, effective 1 November.

## Referral bonus
EUR 1,500 for a successful referral, or EUR 2,500 for engineering roles. Paid after the referred employee completes probation.

## Sabbatical
After **5 continuous years** of service, employees may take up to **4 weeks of unpaid sabbatical**. It can be combined with annual leave. One sabbatical is allowed per 5-year period.
