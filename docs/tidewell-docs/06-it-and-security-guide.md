# IT and Security Guide

**Last reviewed:** 3 February 2026 | **Owner:** Security Lead (Ines Carvalho)

## 1. Company equipment
- Every employee receives a company laptop. Laptops are refreshed every **36 months**.
- Engineering may choose macOS or Linux. Other teams receive Windows.
- Contractors receive loaned equipment, which must be returned within **10 working days** after the contract ends. Contractor accounts are disabled at 23:59 on the contract end date.

## 2. Passwords and access
- The company password manager is mandatory. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
- Multi-factor authentication is required on all systems.
- Access to production systems additionally requires a hardware security key.

## 3. Lost or stolen devices
Report a lost or stolen device **within 1 hour** by emailing security@tidewell.example and posting in the Slack channel #security-urgent. Do not wait until you have searched for the device.

## 4. Personal devices
Personal phones and computers may be used for email and Slack only. They must never be used to access customer data or production systems.

## 5. Removable storage
USB storage is disabled by default. Exceptions must be approved by the Security Lead.

## 6. Phishing
Use the "Report Phish" button in the email client, or forward suspicious messages to phishing@tidewell.example. Do not click links or open attachments first.

## 7. Data retention
- Delivery records are kept for 7 years.
- Customer support chat logs are kept for 18 months.
